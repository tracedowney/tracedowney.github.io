Selected stills

Photography is where attention gets slowed down long enough to become visible.

I used the exports to turn this lane into an actual sequence rather than a placeholder. The page now moves between travel images, architectural detail, quieter found textures, and a few animal portraits that still carry a real point of view.

The goal is not volume. It is choosing frames that can hold their own when everything else on the page stops moving.
