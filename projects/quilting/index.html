Original quilt work

Color carries a lot of the feeling, but the structure underneath still has to hold.

I pulled these selections from the quilt exports so this lane finally shows your own finished work instead of sitting empty. The set leans toward pieces that show range: bold star work, calmer geometry, softer block studies, and quilts that read one way from across the room and another way up close.

What ties them together is the patience in the layout. Even when the pattern gets busy, the balance still feels deliberate.

Starburst layouts Block studies Color rhythm Finished quilt tops

Finished quilts with different kinds of energy.

Some are crisp and graphic, others warmer and more conversational, but all of them show the same steady attention to proportion, repetition, and where color needs to rest.

Quilting is one of the clearest places where order becomes something generous.

I wanted this page to keep that same balance: structured enough to read quickly, but warm enough that the work still feels handmade.
