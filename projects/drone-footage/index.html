Selected aerial reels.

These short edits are the clearest examples of how I want original drone captures to function on the site: controlled, atmospheric, and useful for reading a place.

The goal is not constant motion. It is using height, movement, and sequence to reveal something ground-level images cannot always carry on their own.

Each reel below is short on purpose so the footage stays curated and the page stays readable, while the stills farther down show a little of the raw field material behind them.

Raw field captures behind the edits.

I also pulled a few stills from the original exports so the page shows where the cleaner reels begin: one rural overview, one forest cloudline, and one quieter coastal pass.

What makes a flight worth keeping.

  • Frames that clarify geography instead of just showing altitude.
  • Motion that gives the eye time to read the landscape.
  • Weather and light that add atmosphere without muddying the subject.

From raw pass to shorter reel.

The finished clips above are still the cleaner public-facing work. The stills here make the page feel truer to the capture process behind them.

Drone footage belongs in the record when it adds real context.

If the right angle is the one from above, I want the footage to clarify the place, the scale, or the progression of the work instead of just decorating it.
