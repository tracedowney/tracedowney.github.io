Selected aerial reels.

These short edits are the clearest examples of how I want drone footage to function on the site: controlled, atmospheric, and useful for reading a place.

The goal is not constant motion. It is using height, movement, and sequence to reveal something ground-level images cannot always carry on their own.

Each reel below is short on purpose so the footage stays curated and the page stays readable.

Scale and geography Atmosphere without clutter Place-based documentation Short curated edits

Fog and shoreline

A quieter coastal reel that leans on atmosphere, sea stacks, and gentle motion instead of speed.

Golden hour property context

A warmer rural cut focused on land, layout, and the way late light changes how a place reads from above.

Twilight field study

A dusk reel where the fading light and slower movement do most of the work.

Drone footage belongs in the record when it adds real context.

If the right angle is the one from above, I want the footage to clarify the place, the scale, or the progression of the work instead of just decorating it.
