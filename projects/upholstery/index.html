Original restoration work

Good upholstery starts with seeing what a piece can become.

These projects are about more than changing fabric. They ask for proportion, structure, comfort, and patience with the parts people may never see once the work is finished.

The strongest transformations keep the character of the piece while making it ready to live with again. This page begins with one clear chair restoration, then opens out to a few other finished forms.

One chair, from tired starting point to finished piece.

The red-trimmed chair makes the process easy to read: the original shape is still there, but the finished upholstery gives it a completely new presence in the room.

Different pieces, same attention to form.

Whether the material is leather or a more tailored upholstered finish, the work stays grounded in how the furniture feels, functions, and occupies its space.

Restoration is practical work with a visible payoff.

The goal is furniture that earns another long stretch of everyday use while still looking like it belongs in a room on purpose.
